# Energy saving potential of liquid desiccant in evaporative-cooling-assisted 100% outdoor air system

- **Authors**: Jae-Weon Jeong, Su-Gwang Jeong, Hye-Jin Cho
- **Journal**: Energy, Vol. 59, pp. 725–736
- **Publication Year**: 2013
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.energy.2013.07.018](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.energy.2013.07.018)
- **Keywords / Tags**: #LD_DOAS #DecoupledCooling #LatentLoad #EnergyEfficiency

---

### 1. Executive Summary (BLUF)
- Demonstrates thermodynamic decoupling of sensible and latent cooling loads in commercial building ventilation.
- Liquid desiccant removes the entire outdoor latent load (up to 70-80% of summer ventilation load) without requiring sub-dewpoint air cooling.

### 2. Engineering Takeaways for CrecoPLUS DOAS
- Validates the fundamental thermodynamic advantage of liquid desiccant over DX/chilled-water coil condensation.
- Liquid desiccant handles the latent load while high-temperature cooling handles sensible load, preventing coil frosting and moisture carryover.
