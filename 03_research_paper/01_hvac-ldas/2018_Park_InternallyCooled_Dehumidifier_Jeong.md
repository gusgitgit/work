# Experimental analysis of dehumidification performance of an evaporative cooling-assisted internally cooled liquid desiccant dehumidifier

- **Authors**: Joon-Young Park, Beom-Jun Kim, Soo-Yeol Yoon, Jae-Weon Jeong
- **Journal**: Applied Energy, Vol. 228, pp. 1471–1481
- **Publication Year**: 2018
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.apenergy.2018.10.101](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2018.10.101)
- **Keywords / Tags**: `#InternallyCooled` `#EvaporativeCooling` `#DehumidificationEffectiveness` `#LD_DOAS`

---

### 1. Executive Summary (BLUF)
- Experimentally validates an internally cooled liquid desiccant dehumidifier (ICLDD) paired with evaporative cooling water.
- Proves that in-situ removal of latent heat of absorption maintains a cold solution film, preventing surface vapor pressure rise and boosting dehumidification effectiveness by up to 35% compared to adiabatic contactors.

### 2. Engineering Takeaways for CrecoPLUS DOAS
- Demonstrates why internal cooling is mandatory for liquid desiccant 외조기 (DOAS): Without internal cooling, absorption heat warms the desiccant solution, raising its vapor pressure and killing dehumidification drive.
