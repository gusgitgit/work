# A comprehensive review of dehumidifiers and regenerators for liquid desiccant air conditioning system

- **Authors**: A. Gurubalan, Carey J. Simonson et al.
- **Journal**: Energy Conversion and Management, Vol. 238, 114234
- **Publication Year**: 2021
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.enconman.2021.114234](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enconman.2021.114234)
- **Keywords / Tags**: `#ContactorReview` `#InternallyCooled` `#MembraneContactor` `#PackedBed`

---

### 1. Executive Summary (BLUF)
- Definitive technical review categorizing packed towers, falling-film fin-tubes, and membrane contactors.
- Highlights that internally-cooled fin-tube configurations achieve the highest moisture removal per unit volume with lowest liquid inventory.

### 2. Engineering Takeaways for CrecoPLUS DOAS
- Validates the mechanical design choice: Fin-tube internally cooled contactors provide the optimal balance between high heat/mass transfer area and low air-side pressure drop.
