# Model-based optimal control of a dedicated outdoor air-chilled ceiling system using liquid desiccant and membrane-based total heat recovery

- **Authors**: Gaoming Ge, Fu Xiao, Xinhua Xu
- **Journal**: Applied Energy, Vol. 88, Issue 11, pp. 4180–4190
- **Publication Year**: 2011
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.apenergy.2011.04.045](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2011.04.045)
- **Keywords / Tags**: #OptimalControl #LD_DOAS #ChilledCeiling #ModelBasedControl

---

### 1. Executive Summary (BLUF)
- Formulates a model-based supervisory optimal control framework for dedicated outdoor air systems (DOAS) coupled with liquid desiccant dehumidifiers.
- Reduces daily operating energy consumption by up to 17.5% through dynamic setpoint optimization of solution temperature and concentration based on ambient weather forecasts.

### 2. Engineering Takeaways for CrecoPLUS DOAS
- Demonstrates supervisory control architecture for outdoor air variations (hot/humid summer days vs. mild monsoon days).
