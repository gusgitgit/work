# Study on an internally-cooled liquid desiccant dehumidifier with CFD model

- **Authors**: Yimo Luo, Yi Chen, Hongxing Yang, Jielin Luo
- **Journal**: Applied Energy, Vol. 176, pp. 248–261
- **Publication Year**: 2016
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.apenergy.2016.05.133](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2016.05.133)
- **Keywords / Tags**: `#CFDModel` `#FallingFilm` `#BoundaryLayer` `#LiquidSideResistance`

---

### 1. Executive Summary (BLUF)
- Detailed 2D/3D multiphase CFD model of falling liquid film coupled with air flow in an internally-cooled plate dehumidifier.
- Resolves velocity, temperature, and concentration boundary layers within the liquid film, demonstrating that viscous fluids develop steep internal concentration gradients that slow down dynamic adjustment.

### 2. Engineering Takeaways for CrecoPLUS DOAS
- Explains why high viscosity causes liquid-side mass transfer resistance: Diffusion across the laminar film thickness is the primary rate-limiting step during transient control.
