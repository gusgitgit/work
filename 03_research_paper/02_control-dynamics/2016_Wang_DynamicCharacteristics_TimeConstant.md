# Experimental study of dynamic characteristics of liquid desiccant dehumidification processes

- **Authors**: Lingshi Wang, Fu Xiao, Xiaofeng Niu, Dian-ce Gao
- **Journal**: Science and Technology for the Built Environment, Vol. 23, Issue 1, pp. 127–137
- **Publication Year**: 2017 (Online 2016)
- **DOI**: [10.1080/23744731.2016.1211875](https://doi.org/10.1080/23744731.2016.1211875)
- **Keywords / Tags**: #ExperimentalDynamics #TimeConstant #SettlingTime #StepResponse

---

### 1. Executive Summary (BLUF)
- Reports experimental step-response tests of a counter-flow packed liquid desiccant dehumidifier under changing inlet air and solution conditions.
- Quantifies settling times and time constants for heat and mass transfer processes.

### 2. Key Dynamic & Control Insights
- **Settling Time Trends**: Settling time decreases as air and solution flow rates increase, but increases with packing height / contact volume.
- **Humidity vs. Temperature Response**: Outlet air humidity ratio stabilizes faster than outlet air temperature, because temperature stabilization requires heating/cooling the thermal mass of the packing structure.

### 3. Engineering Takeaways for CrecoPLUS DOAS
- Confirms why high solution circulation rates help minimize dynamic settling time.
- Liquid flow rate should not be throttled down during low load, as lower flow rates drastically increase control settling times.
