# State-space model for transient behavior of membrane-based liquid desiccant dehumidifier

- **Authors**: Wei Li, Ye Yao, Divyanshu Kumar Shekhar
- **Journal**: International Journal of Heat and Mass Transfer, Vol. 144, 118711
- **Publication Year**: 2019
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.ijheatmasstransfer.2019.118711](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijheatmasstransfer.2019.118711)
- **Keywords / Tags**: #StateSpaceModel #TransientBehavior #MembraneDehumidifier

---

### 1. Executive Summary (BLUF)
- Formulates a state-space transient model for counter-flow membrane-based liquid desiccant dehumidifiers with mean relative errors under 1%.
- Investigates step-response dynamics of air/solution temperature and humidity across inputs.

### 2. Key Dynamic & Control Insights
- Response time constant of outlet solution temperature is distinctly larger than that of outlet air temperature and humidity.
- State-space representation facilitates modern multivariable control (e.g., LQR, MPC) for decoupling temperature and humidity control.
