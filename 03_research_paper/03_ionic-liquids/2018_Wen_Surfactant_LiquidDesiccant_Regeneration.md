# Investigation on the regeneration performance of liquid desiccant by adding surfactant PVP-K30

- **Authors**: Tao Wen, Lin Lu, Chuanshuai Dong
- **Journal**: International Journal of Heat and Mass Transfer, Vol. 124, pp. 691–699
- **Publication Year**: 2018
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.ijheatmasstransfer.2018.03.005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijheatmasstransfer.2018.03.005)
- **Keywords / Tags**: `#Surfactants` `#SurfaceTension` `#WettingEnhancement` `#ViscosityMitigation`

---

### 1. Executive Summary (BLUF)
- Explores adding trace polymeric surfactant (PVP-K30) to liquid desiccant solutions to lower surface tension and enhance wettability.
- Significantly improves film spreading and reduces Marangoni film breakdown, increasing mass transfer coefficients by up to 28%.

### 2. Engineering Takeaways for CrecoPLUS DOAS
- Provides a chemical/additive pathway to mitigate CrecoPLUS 5100C surface wetting issues without requiring excessive pumping flow rates.
