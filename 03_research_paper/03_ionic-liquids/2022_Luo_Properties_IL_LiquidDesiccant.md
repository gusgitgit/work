# A state-of-the-art review on the liquid properties regarding energy and environmental performance in liquid desiccant air-conditioning systems

- **Authors**: Jielin Luo, Hongxing Yang
- **Journal**: Applied Energy, Vol. 325, 119853
- **Publication Year**: 2022
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.apenergy.2022.119853](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2022.119853)
- **Keywords / Tags**: #IonicLiquids #FluidProperties #CorrosionFree #NonCrystallizing

---

### 1. Executive Summary (BLUF)
- Benchmark comparative review of liquid desiccants: Halide salts (LiCl, LiBr, CaCl2), Glycols (TEG), and Ionic Liquids (ILs) / Deep Eutectic Solvents (DES).
- Explains why Ionic Liquids solve the fundamental operational barriers that prevented widespread adoption of LiCl: zero metal corrosion, no crystallization at high concentration, zero chemical vapor pressure (no VOC emission or droplet vapor hazard).

### 2. Comparison Summary
- **Vapor Pressure / Volatility**: ILs have negligible vapor pressure, eliminating indoor air contamination.
- **Corrosion**: Non-corrosive to standard copper, aluminum, and stainless steel (unlike LiCl which destroys standard AHU materials).
- **Viscosity**: ILs have 10-30x higher viscosity than aqueous LiCl, representing the primary transport trade-off.

### 3. Engineering Takeaways for CrecoPLUS DOAS
- Proves ionic liquids are the safest long-term fluid for occupied building DOAS applications.
- Confirms the primary engineering challenge is managing high viscosity in fluid transport and heat/mass exchange.
