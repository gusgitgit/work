- **Publication Year**: 2022

- **Keywords / Tags**: #EMIMDEP #Viscosity #Density #ThermophysicalData

### 1. Executive Summary (BLUF)
- Measures density ($\\rho$) and dynamic viscosity ($\\eta$) of pure 1-ethyl-3-methylimidazolium diethyl phosphate ([EMIM][DEP]) and solutions from 293.15 to 338.15 K (20 to 65 °C).

### 2. Key Physical Parameters
- Pure [EMIM][DEP] dynamic viscosity at 25 °C is ~280–320 mPa·s.
- Diluted aqueous mixtures (operational desiccant range, ~70–80 wt%) exhibit viscosities in the range of 20–50 mPa·s.
- Strongly temperature-dependent viscosity: Viscosity drops by >50% as temperature rises from 20 °C to 50 °C.

### 3. Engineering Takeaways for CrecoPLUS DOAS
- Confirms laminar flow conditions in falling film contactors ($\\mathrm{Re} < 20$).
- Regenerator section operating at 55–65 °C benefits from significantly lower liquid viscosity, facilitating faster mass transfer during moisture desorption.
