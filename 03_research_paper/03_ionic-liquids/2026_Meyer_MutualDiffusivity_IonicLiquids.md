# Experimental data on mutual mass diffusivities of two ionic liquids in their aqueous solution obtained by the diaphragm cell method

- **Authors**: Thomas Alexander Meyer et al.
- **Journal**: International Journal of Refrigeration, 107027
- **Publication Year**: 2026
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.ijrefrig.2026.107027](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijrefrig.2026.107027)
- **Keywords / Tags**: #MassDiffusivity #IonicLiquids #StokesEinstein #LiquidSideResistance

---

### 1. Executive Summary (BLUF)
- Measures mutual diffusion coefficients ({AB}$) of ionic liquid aqueous solutions using the diaphragm cell method.
- Experimentally proves that as ionic liquid concentration and viscosity increase, molecular diffusion of water inside the liquid drops by 1 to 2 orders of magnitude ({AB} \\approx 10^{-10} \\sim 10^{-11} \\text{ m}^2/\\text{s}$).

### 2. Engineering Takeaways for CrecoPLUS DOAS
- Directly explains the physical origin of liquid-side mass transfer resistance (/k_L$).
- Proves why static/stagnant films severely retard moisture absorption; falling-film flow must induce surface renewal or wavy laminar ripples to enhance effective mass diffusivity.
