- **Publication Year**: 2021

### 1. Executive Summary (BLUF)
- ORNL study developing an internally-cooled membrane contactor for ionic liquid dehumidifiers.
- Hollow-fiber membranes completely isolate liquid desiccant from the air stream, guaranteeing zero chemical droplet carryover into supply air even at high air face velocities.

### 2. Engineering Takeaways for CrecoPLUS DOAS
- Ideal reference for critical hygiene facilities (hospitals, semiconductor cleanrooms, pharmaceuticals) where even trace aerosol droplets must be 100% prevented.
