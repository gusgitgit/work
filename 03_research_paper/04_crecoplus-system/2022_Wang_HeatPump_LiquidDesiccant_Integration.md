# Review of liquid desiccant air dehumidification systems coupled with heat pump: System configurations, component design, and performance

- **Authors**: Lingshi Wang, Xiaobing Liu et al.
- **Journal**: Energy and Buildings, Vol. 277, 112655
- **Publication Year**: 2022
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.enbuild.2022.112655](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enbuild.2022.112655)
- **Keywords / Tags**: #HeatPumpLDAC #SystemIntegration #LowTemperatureRegeneration

---

### 1. Executive Summary (BLUF)
- Comprehensive review of heat-pump coupled liquid desiccant systems (HP-LDAS).
- Analyzes optimal thermodynamic coupling where heat pump evaporator provides internal cooling (15-20 °C) while condenser heat (50-65 °C) drives desiccant regeneration in a single closed thermal loop.

### 2. Engineering Takeaways for CrecoPLUS DOAS
- Ideal system architecture for CrecoPLUS 5100C DOAS: Use a dedicated DX heat pump package where the evaporator cools the dehumidifier fin-tubes and the condenser warms the regenerator.
- Eliminates the need for external boilers or gas heaters for regeneration.
