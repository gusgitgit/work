# Experimental and modeling study of bubble absorption-based deep dehumidification using the ionic liquid: Parametric analysis on heat and mass transfer

- **Authors**: Bowen Cao, Yonggao Yin, Guoying Xu, Wenzhang Li, Suzhou Dai, Wanhe Chen, Qiang Ji, Fan Zhang
- **Journal**: Energy Conversion and Management, Vol. 288, 117169
- **Publication Year**: 2023
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.enconman.2023.117169](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enconman.2023.117169)
- **Keywords / Tags**: #BubbleAbsorption #ViscosityMitigation #DeepDehumidification #MassTransferEnhancement

---

### 1. Executive Summary (BLUF)
- Addresses the low mass transfer rate caused by high viscosity of ionic liquids by replacing conventional packed beds with a **bubble column absorption contactor**.
- Bubbling the humid air directly through the ionic liquid pool breaks liquid-side diffusion resistance through intense interfacial shear and recirculation.

### 2. Engineering Takeaways for CrecoPLUS DOAS
- Provides an alternative contactor architecture for deep dehumidification applications where falling films suffer from viscous resistance.
