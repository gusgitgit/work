# A Theoretical Model for a Spray-type Ionic Solutions Dehumidifier Driven by Industrial Waste Heat

- **Authors**: Jyun-De Liang, Cheng-Kai Hung
- **Journal**: E3S Web of Conferences, Vol. 634, 01003
- **Publication Year**: 2025
- **DOI**: [10.1051/e3sconf/202563401003](https://doi.org/10.1051/e3sconf/202563401003)
- **Keywords / Tags**: #WasteHeat #IonicSolutionModel #SolutionAirRatio

---

### 1. Executive Summary (BLUF)
- Theoretical and simulation model for ionic liquid dehumidification powered by industrial waste heat.
- Analyzes solution-to-air mass flow ratio (/G$) to optimize dew point suppression while avoiding pump oversizing and excess pumping electricity.

### 2. Engineering Takeaways for CrecoPLUS DOAS
- Provides /G$ ratio selection guidelines to balance pressure drop and dehumidification capacity.
