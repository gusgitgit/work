# Investigation of regeneration performance of an ionic liquid-based dehumidification system operated in two air inlet modes

- **Authors**: Jia-Wei Zheng, Yu-Lieh Wu, Shengjie Li, Chih-Hao Chen, Jiun-Jen Chen
- **Journal**: International Journal of Low-Carbon Technologies, ctaf081
- **Publication Year**: 2025
- **DOI**: [10.1093/ijlct/ctaf081](https://doi.org/10.1093/ijlct/ctaf081)
- **Keywords / Tags**: #CrecoPLUS5100C #Regeneration #AirInletMode #WasteHeat

---

### 1. Executive Summary (BLUF)
- Examines regeneration coil performance of an ionic liquid ([EMIM][DEP] / CrecoPLUS 5100C) dehumidification system operating with ambient outdoor air versus indoor return air.
- Optimizing regeneration coil temperature increases regeneration moisture desorption capacity by up to 69.82%.
- Utilizing indoor return/exhaust air for regeneration increases desorption capacity by 42.66% due to lower initial moisture content.

### 2. Engineering Takeaways for CrecoPLUS DOAS
- DOAS exhaust air should always be routed to the regenerator as scavenging air, dramatically reducing the required regeneration heating water temperature.
