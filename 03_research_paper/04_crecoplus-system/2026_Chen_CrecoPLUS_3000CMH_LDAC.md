### 1. Executive Summary (BLUF)
- Evaluates real-scale pilot performance of a 3,000 CMH (m³/h) Liquid Desiccant Air Conditioning (LDAC) system using newly developed ionic liquid ([EMIM][DEP] / CrecoPLUS 5100C).
- Demonstrates dehumidification performance matching conventional LiCl without metal corrosion or crystallization hazards.

### 2. Key Pilot Findings
- Confirms feasibility of 10-30% energy reduction in independent temperature and humidity control in hot and humid climates.
- Eliminates material compatibility constraints: Standard AHU metals (copper tubes, aluminum fins, galvanized steel casings) can be safely utilized without expensive titanium or plastic linings.
