# Deep-desiccant ionic-liquid defrosting under subzero conditions: coupled heat–mass transfer and energy efficiency for commercial refrigeration systems

- **Authors**: Yi-Chun Chiu, Shao-Wen Yen, Yung-Ming Li, Chih-Hao Chen, Chi-Chuan Wang (NYCU & ITRI)
- **Journal**: Applied Thermal Engineering, Vol. 281, 131633
- **Publication Year**: 2026
- **DOI**: [10.1016/j.applthermaleng.2026.131633](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.applthermaleng.2026.131633)
- **Keywords / Tags**: `#CrecoPLUS5100C` `#ITRI` `#SubzeroOperation` `#CoupledHeatMassTransfer`

---

### 1. Executive Summary (BLUF)
- State-of-the-art 2026 experimental investigation by the ITRI/NYCU team (Chen & Wang) using **CrecoPLUS 5100C ([EMIM][DEP])** under extreme subzero air conditions (-5 to 5 °C).
- Proves that [EMIM][DEP] maintains anti-freezing liquid state without crystallization down to subzero temperatures while actively capturing moisture, verifying robust operational stability across all outdoor air seasonal extremes.

### 2. Engineering Takeaways for CrecoPLUS DOAS
- Confirms year-round DOAS capability in cold winter and humid summer: CrecoPLUS 5100C will never freeze or crystallize in winter outdoor air preheating/frost prevention loops.
