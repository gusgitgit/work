# Liquid Desiccant Assisted Indirect Evaporative Cooling for Data Centers

**Author:** Chen, Y., et al.  
**Year:** 2024  
**Journal:** MDPI Energies  
**DOI:** 10.3390/en17051234  

## 1. BLUF
Liquid desiccant dehumidification can be synergistically integrated with indirect evaporative cooling (IEC) to reduce data center PUE below 1.15 in hot/humid climates without requiring mechanical chillers.

## 2. Core Mechanism
- Data centers traditionally struggle with IEC in humid climates because the wet-bulb temperature of the ambient air is too high.
- By pre-treating the secondary air stream with a liquid desiccant (LiCl or KCOOH), the humidity is drastically lowered, which depresses the wet-bulb temperature.
- This allows the IEC to cool the primary data center air to target temperatures purely through evaporation, turning a sensible cooling problem into a latent mass-transfer solution.

## 3. Findings
- Reduces compressor-based cooling energy by over 60%.
- Regeneration of the liquid desiccant can be powered entirely by low-grade waste heat (e.g., 50-60°C hot water recovered from server cold plates).
