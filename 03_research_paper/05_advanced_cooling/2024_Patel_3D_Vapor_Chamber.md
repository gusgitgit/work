# 3D Vapor Chambers for Two-Phase Immersion Cooling

**Author:** Patel, S., et al.  
**Year:** 2024  
**Journal:** International Journal of Heat and Mass Transfer  
**DOI:** 10.1016/j.ijheatmasstransfer.2024.120987  

## 1. BLUF
Integrating 3D vapor chambers into the heat sinks of chips submerged in dielectric fluids significantly enhances nucleate boiling, increasing the critical heat flux (CHF) limit by 40%.

## 2. Core Concept
- In two-phase immersion cooling, the dielectric fluid boils directly on the chip surface. If the heat flux is too high, a continuous vapor film forms (dryout), insulating the chip and causing rapid failure.
- By using a "Coral-shaped" 3D vapor chamber, the heat from the silicon die is rapidly spread across a much larger, complex surface area.

## 3. Results
- The 3D vapor chamber delays the onset of film boiling, pushing the Critical Heat Flux (CHF) from 100 W/cm² to over 140 W/cm².
- This technology is critical for enabling liquid immersion cooling for next-generation 1000W+ GPUs.
