# Machine Learning-Based Predictive Control for Chilled Water Systems in Semiconductor Fabs

**Author**: Choi, Y., et al.  
**Year**: 2025  
**Category**: Control & Dynamics  
**URL**: [Applied Energy](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2025.123456)

## 📌 Executive Summary (BLUF)
> Using weather forecasts and production schedules, the ML model reduces chiller energy consumption by 18% compared to traditional PID/reactive controls.

## 📖 Detailed Summary
Deep neural networks predict cooling loads in semiconductor fabs 24 hours ahead, optimizing chiller staging and condenser water temperatures.
