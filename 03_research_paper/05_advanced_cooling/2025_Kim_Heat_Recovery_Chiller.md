# System-level Integration of Heat Recovery Chillers in Semiconductor Fabs

**Author:** Kim, D., & Lee, S.  
**Year:** 2025  
**Journal:** Energy and Buildings  
**DOI:** 10.1016/j.enbuild.2025.115432  

## 1. BLUF
Replacing standard cooling towers and gas boilers with heat recovery chillers in semiconductor fabs can reduce Scope 1 carbon emissions by 95% while providing simultaneous cooling and Ultra-Pure Water (UPW) preheating.

## 2. Thermodynamics
- Standard chillers eject condenser heat at around 30-35°C to the atmosphere via cooling towers.
- Heat recovery chillers operate at a higher condensing pressure to produce hot water at 55-65°C.
- While this reduces the cooling COP (from ~6.0 to ~4.5), the *combined* COP (cooling + heating) exceeds 8.0 because the heating energy is essentially free.

## 3. Implementation in Fabs
- UPW systems require heating raw water to 25°C for optimal RO (Reverse Osmosis) membrane performance.
- Directing the 60°C recovered heat to the UPW heat exchangers completely eliminates the need for fossil-fuel boilers.
- Economic analysis shows a payback period of less than 3.5 years when carbon taxes are factored in.
