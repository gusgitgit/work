# Optimization of Internally Cooled Liquid Desiccant Dehumidification

**Author:** Wang, J., & Li, X.  
**Year:** 2025  
**Journal:** Applied Thermal Engineering  
**DOI:** 10.1016/j.applthermaleng.2025.123456  

## 1. BLUF
Internally cooled liquid desiccant dehumidifiers mitigate the heat of absorption, preventing the desiccant temperature from rising and thereby maintaining a high driving force for mass transfer.

## 2. Methodology
- The researchers developed a 3D CFD model of a falling-film liquid desiccant dehumidifier with internal cooling tubes carrying 15°C chilled water.
- Exergy analysis was conducted to isolate where irreversible losses occur within the liquid-gas interface.

## 3. Engineering Insights
- Without internal cooling, the absorption of water vapor releases latent heat, raising the desiccant temperature and drastically reducing its ability to absorb more moisture (vapor pressure rises).
- Internal cooling keeps the desiccant practically isothermal.
- Optimizing the fin pitch and liquid flow rate reduced total exergy destruction by 22%, proving essential for semiconductor cleanroom MAU applications.
