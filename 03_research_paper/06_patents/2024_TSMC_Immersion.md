# US20240098765A1: Immersion Cooling System with Bubble Guidance Structure

**Author**: TSMC  
**Year**: 2024  
**Category**: Patent  
**URL**: [US Patent & Trademark Office](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20240098765A1)

## 📌 Executive Summary (BLUF)
> Prevents 'vapor blanketing' (dryout) on stacked 3D ICs by forcing bubbles into a dedicated exhaust channel, maximizing Critical Heat Flux (CHF).

## 📖 Detailed Summary
A structural manifold inside an immersion cooling tank that directs two-phase vapor bubbles away from neighboring logic chips.
