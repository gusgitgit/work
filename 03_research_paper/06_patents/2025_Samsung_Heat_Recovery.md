# US20250012345A1: Apparatus and Method for Heat Recovery from Semiconductor Manufacturing Equipment

**Author**: Samsung Electronics  
**Year**: 2025  
**Category**: Patent  
**URL**: [US Patent & Trademark Office](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20250012345A1)

## 📌 Executive Summary (BLUF)
> Instead of exhausting heat through cooling towers, this patent describes a cascade heat pump that generates 65C hot water for Make-up Air Unit (MAU) heating, eliminating gas boilers.

## 📖 Detailed Summary
A system for recovering low-grade waste heat from PCW (Process Cooling Water) using a specialized high-lift heat pump.
